## 🌟 Overview

**Hans Credulous** is an all-in-one data-driven exam preparation portal and PYQ (Previous Year Questions) engine engineered for Indian competitive examinations including **SSC**, **Railway (RRB)**, **Banking (IBPS/SBI)**, **Defence (UPSC CDS/NDA)**, and **State Exams**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- 🎯 **High-Yield Topic Focus**: Strategic weightage heatmaps identifying the 50%+ score-yielding topics based on official Tier-1 exam trends.
- ⏱️ **TCS iON CBT Simulator**: Authentic exam runner with countdown timer, sectional marking schemes, and question palette.
- 📓 **Auto Mistake Notebook**: Incorrect answers are automatically cataloged into a dedicated revision notebook for targeted re-testing.
- 💾 **100% Offline Capability**: Download papers and study without internet connectivity.
- 🎨 **Deep Maroon Theme**: Refined `#800000` aesthetic with clean OTP authentication.

## 📚 Covered Examinations

- **SSC**: SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, SSC Stenographer, SSC GD Constable, SSC CPO, SSC MTS & Havaldar, SSC Selection Post, SSC JE, SSC JHT, SSC Delhi Police.
- **Railway (RRB)**: RRB NTPC (Graduate Level 5 & 6), RRB NTPC (Undergraduate 10+2), RRB Group D (RRC Level-1), RRB ALP, RRB Technician, RRB JE, RPF SI, RPF Constable, RRB Paramedical, RRB Senior Section Engineer.
- **Banking**: IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk, SBI PO, SBI Clerk, RBI Grade B, IBPS RRB Officer Scale-I.
- **Defence & State**: UPSC CDS, UPSC NDA & NA, IAF AFCAT, UPSC CAPF AC, CTET, UP Police, Bihar SSC (BSSC CGL).
